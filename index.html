How the Workload Tracker works
1
Once a week, submit two columns of hours. Actual = the hours you really worked in the week just finished. Forecast = the hours you expect to work in the week ahead. Per project or deal — this replaces the old Excel file.
2
The cycle rolls over every Friday at 15:00. From Friday afternoon the Submit tab switches to the new cycle: you log the week that is ending and forecast the next one. Soft deadline: Monday 18:00.
3
Don't retype numbers — copy them. Your Actual is pre-filled from the forecast you made for that week. The “Copy Actual → Forecast” button between the columns does the same in the other direction, so you only adjust the differences.
4
Made a mistake? Just resubmit. Submitting again overwrites your previous numbers. You can edit your own submissions for 14 days; after that, ask a PL. Weeks closed by the Admin (🔒) are locked for everyone.
5
Check yourself in History and Dashboard. History lists every week you've logged and its status. Dashboard shows how your hours split across projects and deals, and how accurate your forecasts turn out to be.
